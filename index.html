About Me

Dedicated to advancing transparent governance, fiscal sustainability, and evidence-driven policymaking.

I am an Economist and development professional serving as Senior Planning Officer at the Kaduna State Planning and Budget Commission. My work is centered on ensuring that public resources translate into measurable societal outcomes through rigorous monitoring, strategic development planning, and macroeconomic research.

Armed with a solid academic background in Economics from Kaduna State University, I have played a pivotal role in formulating and analyzing state-level analytical instruments. I contributed directly to the 2024 Kaduna State Debt Sustainability Analysis (DSA), supported the production of the Kaduna State Macroeconomic Outlook, and participated actively in formulating the State Development Plan and annual budgets.

In the field of Monitoring and Evaluation, I lead tracking missions for priority government projects in education, healthcare, and infrastructure. I deploy technology-driven oversight systems including the Kaduna State Public Expenditure Tracking System (PETS) and the acclaimed Eyes and Ears Project. By monitoring contractor compliance, tracking MDA budget implementation returns, and handling citizen feedback, I ensure that public accountability remains central to state governance.
